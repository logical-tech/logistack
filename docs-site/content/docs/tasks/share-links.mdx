---
title: Read-only share links
description: Share one list or view of tasks with a client who has no account, read-only, with an optional password and expiry, and revoke it at any time.
---

A share link shows one list or one view to someone without a LogiStack account, such as a client who wants to follow progress. The page is read-only and shows only what you choose. If the person should comment, invite them as a [guest](./guests.mdx) instead.

Share links are off by default. A project manager turns them on with **Share links** in the project's **Work** settings. Then anyone with `work` on the project can create one.

## Create a share link

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the wizard

Open the **⋯** menu of a list or view and select **Share link…**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose what to show

Give the link an internal name, such as `Acme weekly`. Choose whether to show assignees (on by default), comments (off by default), and which custom fields.
</Step>
<Step>
### Protect it

Optionally set a password and an expiry date.
</Step>
<Step>
### Copy the link

Check the summary and create the link. The link is shown once. Copy it now and send it to the client.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What the client sees

The link opens `https://<your dashboard>/share/<token>`: the tasks grouped by status, or as a board when the view is a board. Selecting a task shows its description, checklist progress, the fields you allowed, and comments if you turned them on.

- The page works without JavaScript and without an account.
- Search engines are told not to index it, and it sends no referrer to other sites.
- With a password, the client types it once and stays signed in to that link for 12 hours.

## Manage and revoke links

Open the project's **Work** settings and the **Share links** list. Each link shows its name, views and last access. Select **Revoke** to stop a link at once. Creating and revoking links are recorded in the [audit log](../team/audit-log.mdx).
