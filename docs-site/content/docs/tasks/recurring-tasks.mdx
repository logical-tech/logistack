---
title: Recurring tasks
description: Make a task repeat every day, week, month or year, when it is completed or on a fixed schedule, and choose what each new occurrence copies.
---

A recurring task comes back on a rule, such as "every Monday" or "on the 1st of every month". Use it for routine work: a monthly report, a weekly backup check, a yearly domain renewal. Each occurrence is a full task of its own, so its comments and time stay with it.

## Make a task repeat

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the date picker

Open the task and select its dates, or press `d`.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pick a rule

In the task's **Dates** row, select **Doesn't repeat** and pick a preset: **Every day**, **Every weekday**, **Every week on** a day, **Every month on the** n-th day, or **Custom…**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Set the details

In **Custom…** you can set:

| Setting | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Every** | 1 to 365 days, weeks, months or years |
| **On** | For weekly rules, the weekdays |
| **On day** | For monthly rules, a day of the month, or **Last day** |
| **Create the next one** | **When done**, or **On schedule** |
| **Ends** | **Never**, **On date**, or **After** a number of occurrences |

The [API and MCP tools](./api-and-mcp.mdx) also accept the n-th weekday of a month (such as the last Friday), creating scheduled tasks some days before their due date, which parts to copy (subtasks, checklist, field values, assignees, description), and moving dates off non-working days.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save

The task shows ↻ in every view. Hover it to read the rule, such as "Every month on the 1st".
</Step>
</Steps>

## When the next task appears

- **When done**: the next task is created the moment you complete this one, in the same list, with its first active status and the checklist unticked.
- **On schedule**: the next task is created on time, whether or not this one is done. LogiStack checks every hour.

Dates move from the previous occurrence's scheduled dates, not from when it was completed, so a late completion doesn't make the series drift. A monthly rule on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

Reopening a completed occurrence doesn't delete the next one. Each new task links back to the previous one in its history.

## Change or stop a rule

The rule lives on the latest occurrence. Edit it there to change future occurrences. Select **Stop repeating** to end the series. Past occurrences stay as they are.
