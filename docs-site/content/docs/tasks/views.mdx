A view shows a project's tasks, or one list's tasks, in one of four layouts: table, board, timeline or calendar. Each view keeps its own filters, sorting, grouping and columns. Read this page to pick the right layout, to plan a launch on the timeline, or to save a view your team can open every morning.

Every project starts with four shared views: **List** (a table grouped by status), **Board** (a board by status), **Timeline** and **Calendar**. The views appear as tabs at the top of the project. Press `1` to `4` to switch between the first four.

## Filter, sort and group

The bar above every view has the same controls:

- **+ Filter** adds a condition: pick a property (status, assignee, priority, list, tag, dates, created by, milestone, blocked, or a custom field), an operator and a value. Group conditions with **and** and **or**, two levels deep, up to 30 conditions. Assignee and user fields accept **Me**.
- Date filters accept fixed dates or relative ranges: today, yesterday, tomorrow, this week, last week, next week, this month, next 7 days, last 7 days, next 30 days, overdue and no date. Relative ranges use your time zone.
- **Sort** by up to three properties.
- **Group** by status, priority, assignee, list, tag, due date (Overdue, Today, Tomorrow, This week, Next week, Later) or a select field.
- **Show closed** includes tasks in Done and Cancelled statuses.
- **Subtasks**: nested under their parent, flat, or hidden.
- The search box filters by words in the title and description.

Filters you add without saving live in the page URL, so you can send a filtered view as a link.

## Table

{/* screenshot pending until the Work mock fixture lands: ![The List view of project Shop: tasks grouped by status with key, title, assignees, priority, due date and a Client column](/screens/work-table.webp) */}

- Columns: key, title, status, assignees, priority, start, due, tags, list, estimate, tracked time, created, updated, and every custom field. Resize and reorder columns by dragging. The title column stays pinned.
- Click a cell or press `Enter` to edit it, `Esc` to cancel, `Tab` to move to the next cell.
- Each group has a **+ Add task** row. Press `c` to add a task to the current group.
- Check rows, or press `x`, to select them. Shift-click selects a range. A bar appears for bulk changes: status, assignee, priority, dates (set them or shift them by a number of days), tags, list, archive and delete. One bulk edit changes up to 500 tasks.
- With manual sorting, `⌘↑` and `⌘↓` move the focused task.
- **Export CSV** downloads the visible columns with the current filters and sort. See [CSV import and export](./csv-import-and-export.mdx).

## Board

{/* screenshot pending until the Work mock fixture lands: ![The Board view of project Shop with To do, In progress, Review and Done columns, cards showing key, title, assignee avatars, due date and checklist progress](/screens/work-board.webp) */}

- Columns come from the grouping: status (the default), priority, assignee, list, tag, a select field or due date. Dragging a card into a column sets that value. Dropping a card in **Tomorrow** sets its due date to tomorrow.
- A task with two assignees appears in both assignee columns. Dropping it in a third person's column replaces the assignee of the column it came from.
- You can't drag between tag or multi-select columns, because the change would be ambiguous. The cursor shows it.
- Cards show the key, title, assignees, due date (red when overdue), priority, up to six fields you choose, subtask and checklist progress, comment count and a blocked icon. A view can show the first image attachment as a cover.
- Column headers show the count, a **+** to add a task, and turn amber over the status's WIP limit.
- **Swimlanes** split the board into rows by assignee, priority, list or a select field.

To move a card with the keyboard, focus it, press `Space` to lift it, use the arrow keys, and press `Space` to drop it or `Esc` to cancel. Every card's **⋯** menu also has **Move to…**.

## Timeline

{/* screenshot pending until the Work mock fixture lands: ![The Timeline view at week zoom: tasks as bars grouped by list, a milestone diamond for Launch and dependency arrows between design and build tasks](/screens/work-timeline.webp) */}

The timeline shows tasks as bars from their start date to their due date, with the task tree on the left.

- **Zoom**: day, week, month or quarter. Hold `⌘` or `Ctrl` and scroll to zoom around the pointer. **Today** scrolls back to today.
- **Rows** by list, by assignee or flat. Subtasks are indented.
- **Move a task**: drag its bar. **Change its dates**: drag either end. With a bar focused, `←` and `→` move it one day and `Shift+←` and `Shift+→` change its end.
- **Milestones** show as diamonds and have one date.
- A task with only a due date shows as a short dashed bar ending on that date.
- **Unscheduled** lists tasks without dates. Drag one onto the grid to set its start on that day and its due date from its estimate (one working day when it has none).
- Weekends and other non-working days are shaded, from the project's **Working days** setting.

### Dependencies

A dependency says a task can't start until another one finishes. Drag the dot at the end of a bar onto another bar to create one, or press `⌘D` on a focused bar. Select an arrow and press `Delete` to remove it. A dependency can have a lag in days.

- Dependencies are finish-to-start and stay within one project.
- LogiStack refuses a dependency that would create a loop, and names the tasks in it.
- When you move a task past the start of a task that depends on it, a prompt offers **Also move 3 dependent tasks**, with a preview. Dependent tasks move by working days. A dependent task that is already late is never moved earlier.
- Arrows of dependencies that are not respected turn red.

Below 768 pixels wide the timeline is read-only. **List equivalent** opens the same rows as a table, for screen readers and keyboards.

## Calendar

{/* screenshot pending until the Work mock fixture lands: ![The Calendar view in month mode: tasks on their due dates and multi-day tasks as bars across the week](/screens/work-calendar.webp) */}

- **Month** shows six weeks. **Week** shows a column per day, with timed tasks placed at their due time and all-day tasks on top.
- Pick the date the calendar uses: the due date, the span from start to due, or a custom date field.
- Drag a task to another day to reschedule it. A span keeps its length. Drag the ends of a span to change its dates.
- Busy days show **+N more**. The unscheduled sidebar works like the timeline's.
- With the keyboard: arrows move between days, `Enter` lists the day's tasks, `n` creates a task due that day.

## Save a view

When you change a view's filters, sorting, grouping or columns, the bar shows **Save view**:

- **Save** updates the view for everyone who sees it.
- **Save as new** creates another view.
- **Reset** goes back to the saved settings.

To add a view, select **+ View**. The wizard asks for the layout, a name, whether it is **Shared** or **Personal**, and a starting filter.

| View | Who sees it | Who can change it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared | Everyone with `read:work` on the project | `work:admin` |
| Personal | Only you | You, with `work` |

A project holds up to 50 shared views, and each person up to 20 personal views. A view can cover the whole project or one list.
