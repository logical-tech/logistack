Everything about who can reach your LogiStack organization and what they can do there lives under **Settings** in the sidebar, plus **Account and security** in your user menu. Read this section when you add a teammate, connect CI or an AI agent, plug in your company's identity provider, or need to answer "who changed this?".

## How access works

Every person and every machine acts through a **role**, and every role is granted on a **scope**:

- A **role** is a set of actions: Read, Read secrets, Deploy, Write, Delete, Administer and Terminal. LogiStack ships four built-in roles (Owner, Admin, Developer, Viewer) and you can add your own.
- A **scope** is the whole organization, a folder, a project, an environment or a single service. A role on a folder reaches every project, environment and service inside it.
- Access only adds up. A member's organization role applies everywhere, and extra grants widen it on one part of the tree. Nothing ever takes access away.
- API keys act as their owner, optionally narrowed to some scopes. A key never does more than the person or service account that owns it.

```text title="Example: one organization, three people"
Organization "Logical"
├── Giulia      Admin on the organization        → manages everything
├── Marco       Viewer on the organization
│               + Developer on folder Clients     → deploys anything under Clients
└── Tom         Viewer on the organization
                + deployer on project Storefront   → deploys Storefront only
```

## Who can change these settings

Only **Owners** and **Admins** of the organization manage members, roles, SSO, the 2FA requirement, service accounts and SSH keys, and read the audit log. Everyone can manage their own API keys, passkeys, two-factor and sessions, and pick their own theme.
