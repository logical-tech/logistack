# Code of conduct

LogiStack is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. This code applies to every LogiStack space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any chat or event where you represent the project.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Critique work, not people. Say what is wrong and why, and suggest what would be better.
- Welcome questions, including basic ones. Everyone started somewhere.
- Respect people's time: search before you ask, give the details a reader needs, and follow up on what you open.
- Talk about other projects, including Dokploy and Coolify, with respect. LogiStack owes them ideas.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Comments that demean anyone for their identity, background, body, ability, age, or experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwanted attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion, or encouraging others to do it.

## How to report

Email `info@logicaltech.it` with `[conduct]` in the subject. Say what happened, where, and who was involved; links or screenshots help. Only the maintainers read these reports, and we keep them confidential.

We reply within a week. We may ask for more details, and we tell you what we decided.

## What we do

Depending on what happened, the maintainers may:

1. Explain privately what was wrong and ask for a change.
2. Give a public warning.
3. Remove comments, lock a thread, or ban someone from the project's spaces for a time or for good.

We act on behaviour, not on who someone is. A maintainer who is part of a report takes no part in deciding it.
